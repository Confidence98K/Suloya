About Suloyal

Connecting businesses.
Creating global opportunities.

Since 2011, Suloyal International has helped businesses connect with reliable manufacturers, products and markets across borders — from a Hong Kong trading office to an international sourcing, trade and supply-chain network.

Company overview

Founded in Hong Kong. Built for global trade.

Founded in Hong Kong and expanded through Guangzhou, Suloyal has developed into an international sourcing, trading and supply-chain partner serving businesses across more than 50 countries and five continents.

Today, our operations combine sourcing expertise, international trade, logistics capabilities and technology to make global commerce more convenient, efficient and reliable.

Our story

Built for a more connected world.

Suloyal's journey began in 2011 with the establishment of Suloyal Trading Limited in Hong Kong. As our international operations grew, we expanded into mainland China, then into Europe, and most recently strengthened our supply-chain capabilities in Guangzhou. Today, Suloyal brings these capabilities together to connect suppliers, businesses and markets across the world.
